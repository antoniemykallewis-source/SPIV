Eric “Motivate” Spivak is an educator, trusted advisor and key opinion leader — a dot connector, an expeditor, a bridge.

Left home at 18 with no formal education and no family money. Worked the counter at Papa Johns on Long Island. Wound through Austin. Landed in Los Angeles and got to work: a creative agency and marketing firm known for making the impossible happen, a decade and a half of underground nightlife, five hundred productions, fifteen hundred artists booked and paid.

Specialties, if you're keeping the sheet: marketing, consulting, business development, partnerships, product design, community building, creative strategy, special events & experiential, product management, growth & retention.

Featured in Rolling Stone, GQ, Complex and Decrypt. Collaborated with LVMH Group, Netflix, Sotheby's. In 2020 he founded the world's largest and fastest-growing NFT/Web3 community — and served as Twitter's expert for their first official blockchain partnership (2/2/22).

Actively working toward legacy: leading with philanthropy, building longevity, sustainability and accessibility for communities and cultures worldwide.
